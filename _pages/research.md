My research program develops geospatial, building-energy, and life-cycle assessment methods to understand and accelerate urban transitions toward net-zero and low-carbon cities.

## Research areas

### Urban Building Energy & Net-Zero Transitions

Urban building energy modelling, building archetypes, retrofit, electrification, solar energy, district energy, and net-zero pathways.

### Geospatial & Data-Driven Urban Modelling

GIS, remote sensing, LiDAR, 2D/3D city models, open data, spatial analysis, and GeoAI/data-driven methods.

### Life-Cycle Carbon & Environmental Assessment

Spatial LCA, dynamic LCA, embodied and operational impacts, BIM-LCA, uncertainty, and environmental assessment.

### Climate-Responsive Urban Technologies

Green and cool roofs, solar technologies, urban greening, microclimate, passive strategies, and climate resilience.
