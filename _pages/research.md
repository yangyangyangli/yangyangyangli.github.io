  <div class="research-intro">
    <p>I develop geospatial, urban building energy, and life-cycle assessment methods to understand how buildings and urban systems use energy, generate environmental impacts, and respond to low-carbon interventions. My work connects spatial data, 2D/3D city models, building-energy modelling, and life-cycle assessment to support evidence-based urban decision-making.</p>

    <article class="research-direction" aria-labelledby="research-future-geoai-title">
      <h3 id="research-future-geoai-title">GeoAI-integrated urban energy and life-cycle data systems</h3>
      <p>I aim to integrate GeoAI, UBEM, and LCA in updateable urban data systems, connecting climate projections, land use, building and infrastructure characteristics, energy demand, carbon inventories, and monitoring data to assess energy profiles and climate risks across cities and communities.</p>

    <article class="research-direction" aria-labelledby="research-future-pathways-title">
      <h3 id="research-future-pathways-title">Integrated low-carbon technology and design pathways</h3>
      <p>I aim to compare renewable energy, district energy, urban greening, retrofit strategies, and material choices using embodied carbon, operational energy, cost, service life, and resilience criteria to support transparent design and asset-management decisions.</p>
      <figure class="research-svg" style="width: auto; max-width: 100%; margin-inline: 0; box-sizing: border-box;">

    <article class="research-direction" aria-labelledby="research-future-climate-title">
      <h3 id="research-future-climate-title">Climate-responsive building and infrastructure transitions</h3>
      <p>I aim to connect GIS, BIM, monitoring and sensing, field and laboratory observations, physics-based simulation, spatial prediction, and uncertainty analysis through modular, reproducible Python workflows that can update assessments as climate, population, and policy conditions change.</p>
