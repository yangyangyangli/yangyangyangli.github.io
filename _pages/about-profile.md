  <h2 id="profile-title">Research profile</h2>

  <div class="profile-intro">
    <p>
      Yang Li, PhD, is a researcher working at the intersection of geospatial technologies, urban building energy modelling, and life-cycle assessment. His
      research develops spatial and data-driven approaches to support net-zero and low-carbon transitions in buildings, communities, and cities.
    </p>

    <p>
      His current research interests include GeoAI, GIS, remote sensing, urban building energy modelling (UBEM), spatial life-cycle assessment, urban green
      space, district energy, retrofit and electrification strategies, and renewable-energy integration.
    </p>
  </div>
